The Bobi identity lockup — put it in app chrome, sidebars, login screens, and footers.

```jsx
<BobiLockup fontSize={27} />
<BobiLockup fontSize={22} ink="var(--bobi-ink)" accent="var(--bobi-acc)" />
```

Established sizes: **22px** footer, **27px** header/sidebar, **40px** large footer, **clamp(44px, 7vw, 64px)** hero.

Any surface carrying the lockup must also carry the **"BY MODA LABS ↗"** byline (11px mono, 0.14em, 55% paper, hairline divider to its left) — the attribution is required, not decorative.
